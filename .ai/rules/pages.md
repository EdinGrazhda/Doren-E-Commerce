# Pages

## Admin banners manage only hero carousel content
The Banners admin is intentionally limited to main homepage hero slides. Admins may change only each slide's title, description, and uploaded image; position, active state, order, CTA labels/URLs, the announcement bar, and the lower campaign are not editable here. New hero slides are active and appended automatically.
